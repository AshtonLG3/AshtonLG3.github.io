Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend ordering at least 2 weeks in advance for celebration cakes, and 1-2 months for wedding cakes to ensure availability.

Do you deliver?

Yes! We offer delivery within Lichtenburg for a small fee. Collection from 19C Hamman Street is free.

Do you cater for allergies?

Please see our disclaimer below. While we take care, our kitchen handles nuts, dairy, and wheat, so we cannot guarantee 100% allergen-free products.
